Introduction & About Us

Kasun Enterprises is a manufacturer of shoes and shoe components, equipped with cutting-edge machinery and facilities.

Throughout our nearly two decades in operation, we have established a robust presence in the industry, renowned for delivering superior products, pioneering designs, and unwavering business ethics. At the core of our operations lie principles of sustainability, innovation, and adaptability, serving as the pillars of our success.

Driven by a commitment to excellence, we continually invest in state-of-the-art technology and modern machinery to uphold the highest standards in our field.


Established in 2005, we operate as a sole proprietorship, with our primary headquarters and flagship manufacturing facility located at No. 54, Heraliyawala Road, Polgasowita. Additionally, our secondary manufacturing unit, specializing in shoe sole and safety shoe production, is situated at No. 40, Uduwagawatta Road, Undurugoda, Polgasowita.

Distinguished as one of only two Shoe Last manufacturing entities in Sri Lanka, we take pride in our diverse product range, which includes PVC, TPR, and PU soles supplied to various footwear manufacturers.

From humble beginnings with just five employees, Kasun Enterprises has expanded significantly, now creating over 60 direct employment opportunities and impacting a hundred more indirectly.

Production Capacity

Established in 2005 as a modest structure, our primary factory premises have undergone significant expansion to accommodate our flourishing gents' leather shoe manufacturing division alongside our shoe last manufacturing division.

Additionally, our secondary site houses our PU and PVC manufacturing facility, specializing in the production of shoe outer soles, sandals, and safety shoes.
